[PENGAYAAN] where_sql.py — pohon -> klausa WHERE SQL parameterized
input contoh.filter:
  status = "open" and (priority > 2 or owner = "budi") and
  not archived = true and title contains "invoice" and
  state in ("new", "open", "pending") and score >= 4.5
output:
  WHERE: ((((status = ? AND (priority > ? OR owner = ?)) AND
  NOT (archived = ?)) AND title LIKE ?) AND state IN (?, ?, ?))
  AND score >= ?
  PARAMS: ['open', 2, 'budi', True, '%invoice%', 'new', 'open',
  'pending', 4.5]

cocok, tidak ada nilai literal yang masuk ke teks SQL, tanda kurung
input dipertahankan, LIKE membungkus nilai dengan %...%.
